Step 1 of 5

When does your evening start and end?

Everything is measured against these two numbers. The plan runs from the moment you sit down to the moment you've said you're going to bed — and it never plans past it.

Optional. Used once, in the welcome-home line.

The hard edge. Work that doesn't fit before this gets named, not squeezed.

Where it lives

It does not take over your new tab page. New tabs stay new tabs. Close the pinned tab whenever you want — it comes back on the next browser start, and you can turn that off here or in Options.

Step 2 of 5

The thinking

This is the part that reads your screenshots, pulls tasks out of messy assignment pages, turns what you said out loud into a list, and answers the open-ended questions.

Stored in this browser only. It is never written to the sync database, so it doesn't travel to your phone. Calls go straight from here to the provider, billed to your own account.

If the id is wrong, Test says so immediately and you can change it — nothing else in the app breaks.

You can skip this entirely. Without a key you still get the whole scheduler, the deadline ordering, Future Me pace tracking, voice-to-list, PDF and text capture, and seven lookup questions. You lose screenshot reading and open-ended questions.

Step 3 of 5

Say your list instead of typing it

Talk normally — "biology chapter four review thirty five minutes, then FFA quiz prep forty five minutes, then finish the github project" — and it comes out as separate lines with the times already filled in.

    The transcription happens in the browser — no audio is uploaded. If you set a key on the last step, it also gets a shot at splitting the lines better, and anything it returns that doesn't quote what you actually said gets thrown out.

    Step 4 of 5 · optional

    Your calendar

    Calendar does exactly two things here: it takes time away from the evening, and it flags a deadline when an event name matches something on your list. It never creates work.

    Google Calendar → hover your calendar → ⋮ → Settings and sharing → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. Read-only, no sign-in, works on your phone too. Anyone with the link can read your calendar, so keep it to yourself.

    Your phone

    This is the bit that makes the 3:00 dump land on your PC. Both devices read and write one record; whichever was saved last wins.
